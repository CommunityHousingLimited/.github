# Code of Conduct

Community Housing Limited expects contributors to engage respectfully, professionally and inclusively.

Our values guide how we work:

- **People First**
- **Brave Enough to Lead**
- **Built on Integrity**
- **Relentless in Delivery**

Contributors must comply with applicable CHL policies, workplace requirements, legal obligations and repository-specific instructions.

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, deliberate disruption, misuse of confidential information and other inappropriate conduct are not acceptable.

CHL team members should raise conduct concerns through the appropriate internal management or People & Culture channels. External contributors can contact CHL through https://chl.org.au/contact/.
